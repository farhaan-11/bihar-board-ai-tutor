# Zuno — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2 November 2026**

> tumhare study sawaal-jawaab save karte hain taaki Zuno tumhari padhai me madad
> kar sake. Hum tumhara data **bechte nahi** aur ads ke liye use nahi karte. Jab
> chaaho, email karke apna data **delete** karwa sakte ho. Agar tum 18 se kam ho,
> ye page apne parent ya guardian ke saath padho.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Zuno collects, why, and what choices
you have. Zuno is an AI study tutor for Bihar Board Class 10 Science students.

## 1. What we collect

- **If you create an account:** your name and email address.
- **Your study chats:** the questions you ask and Zuno's answers, so you can see
  your history and track your progress.
- **If you use Zuno as a guest** (without an account): only your questions and a
  random guest id — no name or email.

We do **not** ask for your phone number, gender, photo, home address, or any
payment details.

## 2. Why we collect it

- **Name and email** — to create your account, let you log in, and send account
  emails (like email verification and password reset).
- **Study chats** — to answer your questions, show your chat history, and track
  which topics you have studied.

We do **not** use your information for advertising.

## 3. Who we share it with

To run Zuno, we use a few trusted services. Your information passes through them
only to make Zuno work:

- **OpenAI** — your questions are sent here to generate answers.
- **Google** — if you log in with Google, and as a backup that helps process
  questions when our main provider is down.
- **MongoDB Atlas** — stores your account and chat data.
- **Our email service** — sends account emails (verification, password reset).
- **Hosting and caching services (Render, Vercel, Upstash)** — run the app.

We do **not** sell your information to anyone, and we do **not** share it for
advertising.

## 4. How long we keep it

- While your account is active, we keep your data.
- If your account is unused for **more than 6 months**, we may delete your data at
  any time after that. This is done by hand, so it may take days or months.
- Guest data is minimal (just questions and a random id) and is not linked to your
  name or email.

## 5. Your choices

- You can ask us to **delete** your account and data.
- You can ask to **see or correct** the information we hold about you.

To do any of these, email us at **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]**. We do this by hand, so please
allow a little time.

## 6. Children and minors

Zuno is made for Class 10 students, who are often under 18. If you are under 18,
please use Zuno only with the knowledge and agreement of a parent or guardian. We
collect as little information as possible, and we **never** track children for
advertising. A parent or guardian can email us at **[SUPPORT_EMAIL]** to see or
delete their child's data.

## 7. How we protect your data

Passwords are stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, never as plain text. Data
travels over a secure (HTTPS) connection. No system is perfectly safe, but we take
reasonable care.

## 8. Cookies and local storage

Zuno uses your browser's local storage only to keep you signed in and remember
your session. We do **not** use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top
will change, and big changes will be shown in the app.

## 10. Contact

Questions about your privacy, or want your data deleted? Email us at
**[SUPPORT_EMAIL]**.
